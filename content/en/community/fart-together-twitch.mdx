## What the Fart Together Twitch Category Actually Is

Fart Together is scheduled to launch into Steam Early Access on October 29, 2026, and it already has its own directory page on Twitch. At the time this was written, the [Fart Together Twitch category](https://www.twitch.tv/directory/category/fart-together) showed a modest footprint: 6 viewers, 64 followers, and a handful of small live channels. That's a launch-day snapshot, not a verdict — but it tells you exactly what kind of Twitch presence to expect right now.

The category is filed under Driving/Racing, Adventure, Indie, Arcade, and Action. Twitch's own summary describes it as a physics-based parkour game where one to four players steer the same boat using fart power, coordinating direction, timing, and gas output to clear obstacle courses.

## Who's Streaming Fart Together Right Now

The channels surfaced in the category were small — single-digit viewer counts across the board. Titles were mostly in Russian, including streams from T1mka_29, silentkidyyy, h4zz, and NekStream__. None of them had more than three viewers when the page was captured.

What that means practically:

- **There's no "big streamer" wave yet.** Discovery happens at the bottom of the directory right now.
- **Chat is tiny.** If you join a stream, you're likely one of a few people, which makes asking questions about controls and courses realistic.
- **The demo is doing a lot of the work.** Steam offers a downloadable Fart Together demo, so many early streams are probably people testing before buying.

Viewer and follower counts change by the hour on Twitch. Treat any specific number here as a timestamp, not a trend.

## What Viewers Actually See on Stream

GamesMrkt, the developer and publisher, built Fart Together around one idea: everyone on the raft shares the consequences. In co-op, every player's input moves the same vessel, so a mistimed burst doesn't just cost you — it sends the whole group off course.

The studio's own store description puts it bluntly: the secret isn't farting harder, it's farting together at the right time. Courses include narrow passages, sharp turns, moving obstacles, and hazards that knock the raft off balance. Sometimes the team needs to speed up, sometimes slow down, and sometimes everyone has to commit at the exact same moment.

For stream purposes, that's a reliable recipe for the thing Twitch actually rewards: audible blame. One player going the wrong direction can flip a calm run into an argument about physics.

### The "sort of realistic" raft physics

GamesMrkt is upfront that the physics aren't a simulation — the store page jokes that they probably shouldn't even claim otherwise. What the raft does respond to is force, direction, and timing from each player. On stream, that reads as sudden, physics-driven launches that are hard to predict and easy to laugh at.

## Twitch vs. Steam: Two Slightly Different Descriptions

The two platforms don't describe Fart Together identically, and the gap is worth knowing before you click a stream expecting one thing.

| Detail | Twitch category page | Steam store page |
|---|---|---|
| Player count | 1–4 players | 2–4 players |
| Vehicle wording | "boat" | "raft" |
| Genre wording | Physics-based parkour | Physics-based obstacle course |
| Categories/tags | Driving/Racing, Adventure, Indie, Arcade, Action | Action, Adventure, Casual, Indie, Racing, Free To Play, Early Access |

Neither description is wrong so much as loose. The player-count difference matters most: Steam's co-op framing is 2–4, while Twitch's blurb mentions solo play. If solo play is your deciding factor, confirm it on the [official Fart Together Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5117550/Fart_Together/) rather than a category summary.

Steam also lists the game among Free To Play and Early Access genres, while the developers say they plan to price Early Access lower than the eventual full release. Pricing details are best checked directly on the store page.

## Tips for Watching or Streaming Fart Together

If you're tuning in, lower your expectations for production value and raise them for chaos. The game's appeal on stream is entirely in the group dynamic.

- **Look for streams with two or more players on mic.** A solo run shows the physics; a co-op run shows the arguments.
- **Filter by language.** The category had a strong Russian-language presence on day one, so use Twitch's language filter if you want English commentary.
- **Watch for course variety.** GamesMrkt says Early Access will expand with more courses, obstacles, game modes, characters, and customization over an expected two to three months.
- **Check system requirements before streaming.** Minimum is a GTX 1050 Ti or RX 560 with 8 GB RAM; recommended is a GTX 1660 or RX 590 with 16 GB. A stable connection is required for online co-op.

If you plan to stream it yourself, note that Steam lists Online Co-op, Steam Cloud, and Family Sharing as features, and that Steam Deck compatibility is currently marked "Unknown."

## The Studio Behind the Raft

GamesMrkt describes itself as a small independent studio that has been developing games since 2018, with prior projects reaching millions of players across platforms. Fart Together is its first Steam release. The team says the first version came together over two intense months of tuning raft physics, designing courses, and — in their words — testing hippo farts.

They also confirm the original idea came from the toilet, and decline to elaborate. That's the tone of the whole project: self-aware, deliberately silly, and built for clips.

## FAQ

### Is there a Fart Together Twitch category?

Yes. The game has a dedicated directory page on Twitch with its own viewer and follower counts, filed under Driving/Racing, Adventure, Indie, Arcade, and Action. It's small but live, and it should grow as more players pick up the Early Access build.

### How many players can join a run?

Steam describes Fart Together as a 2–4 player co-op game where everyone steers a shared raft. Twitch's category blurb says 1–4 and mentions playing alone. Since the two sources disagree, check the current store page before planning a solo session.

### Is Fart Together free?

Steam lists Free To Play among the game's genres and also flags it as Early Access, while the developers say they intend to keep the Early Access price below the full release price and to give Early Access owners the full version at no extra cost. Confirm the current price on the Steam listing.

### Can I try it before watching a full stream?

Yes. A Fart Together demo is available to download on Steam, which is the simplest way to feel the raft physics yourself before committing to a purchase or a stream slot.
