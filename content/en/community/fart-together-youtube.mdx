## What the Gameplay Videos Reveal About the Loop

The trailers and the Steam copy tell a consistent story, and it's worth knowing before you judge any clip you find. The core idea is that every player's input moves the whole raft, so a single mistimed burst sends the team off course. The developers sum it up with a line that doubles as the game's design thesis: the trick isn't farting harder, it's farting together at the right moment.

That makes for good video, because failure is funny and readable. A clip where the raft spins into a hazard needs no explanation — you can see exactly whose input ruined the run. It also means the game is best watched with a group, since the comedy comes from blame, not from spectacle.

If you're evaluating whether to buy in during Early Access, watch the trailer with three questions in mind: Does the raft read clearly on screen? Can you tell which player did what? Does the course look like it rewards planning or reflexes? Those answers will tell you more than any hype clip.
