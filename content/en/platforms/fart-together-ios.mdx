## What Fart Together Actually Is, According to the Steam Page

[Fart Together on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5117550/Fart_Together/) is described by developer and publisher GamesMrkt as a physics-based obstacle course game for two to four players. Everyone stands on the same raft and steers it using fart power, coordinating direction, timing, and gas output to clear courses.

The official description is blunt about the design philosophy: the secret isn't farting harder, it's farting together at the right time. Key verified details include:

- **Player count:** 2–4 players in online co-op
- **Core challenge:** sharp turns, narrow passages, moving obstacles, and hazards that knock the raft off balance
- **Tone:** comedy party game with stylized animal characters
- **Steam features:** Online Co-op, Steam Cloud, and Family Sharing
- **Interface languages:** 17 total, including English, French, German, Spanish (Spain), Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, and Portuguese (Brazil)
- **Controller support:** Xbox and PlayStation controllers are listed
- **Reviews:** no user reviews at the time this was written

The developer also notes that the original concept came from the toilet, which tells you most of what you need to know about the humor.

## Why There's No iOS Version to Point To Yet

It's tempting to speculate about a phone port, but the published roadmap doesn't support it. In the Early Access section of the store page, GamesMrkt explains that the Early Access period is meant to refine teamwork, physics, controls, and difficulty with player feedback. The stated plan for the full version is more courses, obstacles, modes, characters, and customization — plus balance, performance, and polish work.

The studio describes itself as a small independent team that has been developing games since 2018, and Fart Together is its first Steam release, built from an initial version created over roughly two chaotic months. Nothing in that description suggests spare capacity for a simultaneous mobile build.

Pricing follows the same Early Access logic: the developer says the game will cost less during Early Access and more at full release, with Early Access buyers receiving the full version at no extra charge. No price figures are listed on the page.

So the accurate framing is this: an iOS version is **unconfirmed and unannounced**, not ruled out. Treat any claim of a release date, App Store page, or mobile beta as unverified until it appears on an official channel.
