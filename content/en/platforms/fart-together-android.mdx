## What the Confirmed PC Version Actually Includes

Understanding the PC build helps explain why a straight port isn't automatic. Fart Together is a physics-based obstacle course in which every player on the raft contributes force, direction, and timing. Narrow passages, sharp turns, and moving hazards punish anyone who acts a beat late. The studio's own framing is that the skill isn't pushing harder — it's syncing up.

Other confirmed details from the Steam listing:

- **Online co-op** is a listed feature, and a stable broadband connection is required for it.
- **Accessibility options** include camera comfort settings, custom volume controls, stereo sound, a keyboard-only option, and no quick-time events.
- **Steam Cloud** support is listed, so progress can carry between PCs.
- **Storage footprint** is small — 4 GB — with a minimum of 8 GB RAM and a GTX 1050 Ti–class GPU.

That last point is worth noting: the requirements are modest by PC standards, but they still describe a desktop-class build, not a mobile one.
