## Is There a Fart Together Mobile Version?

Short answer: no. As of October 29, 2026, the only verified platform for *Fart Together* is Windows PC through Steam, where the game enters Early Access on that same date. The official [Fart Together store page on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5117550/Fart_Together/) lists Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit) as the supported operating systems and says nothing about iOS or Android.

That matters because the keyword "Fart Together mobile" suggests a lot of people are hunting for a phone version that doesn't exist yet. If you're one of them, here's the practical takeaway: you can't download *Fart Together* from the App Store or Google Play today, and no mobile port has been announced by developer GamesMrkt. Anyone claiming otherwise — or offering an APK — is not backed by anything the studio has published.

One small clarification that trips people up: Steam's store page includes a "Get the Steam Mobile App" link at the top. That's Valve's shopping and chat app for browsing the store, not a playable version of the game. Browsing *Fart Together* on your phone is not the same as playing it on your phone.

## What Fart Together Actually Is

*Fart Together* is a physics-based co-op obstacle course game for **2–4 players** who all steer a single shared raft using fart power. Instead of piloting your own vehicle, you and your friends control one craft together, and every input affects the whole team.

The core loop, according to the developers:

- Players coordinate **direction, timing, and gas power** to navigate courses.
- Courses include narrow passages, sharp turns, moving obstacles, and hazards that knock the raft off balance.
- Success depends on acting at the same moment rather than mashing buttons harder — the studio's own tagline is that the secret isn't farting harder, it's farting together at the right time.
- A single mistimed input can send the whole raft off course, which is where the "this may test your friendships" framing comes from.

It's a party-style comedy game with a stylized, colorful 3D look and a top-down/third-person camera. Developer and publisher are the same: **GamesMrkt**, a small independent studio that has been building games since 2018 and describes *Fart Together* as its first Steam release. The team says the first playable version came together over roughly two months of development, including raft physics tuning and course design.

## How to Play Fart Together Right Now

If you want to get on the raft before or at launch, here's what's actually available:

- **Download the demo.** The Steam page hosts a free *Fart Together* demo, which is the lowest-risk way to test the physics and see whether your group can steer straight.
- **Wishlist the game.** Since the page was counting down hours rather than showing a purchase button at the time of writing, wishlisting is how you get notified the moment Early Access opens.
- **Plan for online co-op.** The listed feature set is Online Co-op, Steam Cloud, and Family Sharing. No local split-screen or couch co-op mode is mentioned, so each player should expect to be on their own PC with a broadband connection.
- **Check your controllers.** The page lists support for Xbox and PlayStation controllers, plus a keyboard-only option and a note that the game is playable without quick-time events. Accessibility features include camera comfort settings, custom volume controls, and stereo sound.

Because the game is cross-platform only in the sense that it exists on one platform, everyone in your group needs a Windows PC — you can't mix a PC player with a phone player.

## FAQ

**Is Fart Together available on mobile?**

No. There is no verified Fart Together mobile release on iOS or Android. The game is built for Windows PC and distributed through Steam, with an Early Access launch scheduled for October 29, 2026.

**Can I play Fart Together on my phone through an app or emulator?**

Nothing official supports that. The developers haven't published a mobile build, and the Steam Mobile App only lets you browse and buy games — it doesn't run them. Be cautious with any third-party APK or "mobile port" claiming to be the game.

**How many players can join a Fart Together session?**

Two to four players share one raft in online co-op. Every player's timing and direction input affects the whole team, so the game is designed around voice coordination rather than solo skill.

**What do I need to play Fart Together on PC?**

A 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 machine, 8 GB of RAM minimum (16 GB recommended), a GTX 1050 Ti or RX 560-class GPU, 4 GB of storage, and a stable broadband connection for online co-op. A free demo is available on Steam if you want to test performance before buying.
