## Is Fart Together on PS5? The Short Answer

No PS5 version of Fart Together has been confirmed. As of October 29, 2026, the only first-party storefront page available for the game is its [Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5117550/Fart_Together/), and that listing describes a Windows PC release — nothing more.

That's the honest answer, and it matters because "Fart Together PS5" is a natural search for anyone who saw a clip of four hippos arguing over raft physics and assumed it was a console party game. The trailer energy fits a living room. The paperwork doesn't — at least not yet.

Below is what the official material actually verifies, what it leaves open, and how to keep track of a possible PlayStation release without falling for rumors.

## What the Official Steam Listing Confirms

Fart Together is a physics-based co-op obstacle course game from developer and publisher GamesMrkt, a small independent studio that has been making games since 2018. Two to four players share a single raft and steer it by farting — coordinating direction, timing, and gas power to clear narrow passages, sharp turns, and moving hazards.

Notice what's missing from that table: any mention of PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, or Xbox consoles as release platforms. The system requirements are Windows-only, and the game's online co-op requires a broadband connection.

## PlayStation Controllers Are Not the Same as a PS5 Version

This is the detail that trips people up. The Steam page's accessibility and input section references both Xbox controllers and PlayStation controllers, which means the PC version supports PlayStation gamepads as input devices.

That is controller compatibility on PC — not a PlayStation 5 port. It's an easy thing to misread when you're skimming a store page for platform logos, and it's likely part of why "Fart Together PS5" searches exist at all.

So if you own a DualSense and a gaming PC, you have a reasonable path to playing with the controller you already like. If you own only a PS5, the verified material currently offers you nothing to install.

## What Would Have to Change Before a PS5 Release Exists

GamesMrkt has been explicit that Fart Together is entering Early Access as a work in progress. The studio's stated plan is to spend roughly two to three months refining teamwork, physics, controls, and difficulty with player feedback, then expand the full version with more courses, obstacles, game modes, characters, and customization options.

Console versions aren't mentioned anywhere in that roadmap. That's a meaningful gap rather than a hidden hint — the developer FAQ covers pricing, timeline, and community feedback in detail, and platform expansion simply isn't on the list.

A PS5 release would require an announcement from GamesMrkt or a PlayStation Store listing. Neither exists in the available material. Treat any claim otherwise as unconfirmed until you can trace it back to the developer.
