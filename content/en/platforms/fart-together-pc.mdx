## Fart Together PC: What You're Actually Getting

Fart Together is a physics-based co-op obstacle course game where two to four players steer a single shared raft using fart power, and it arrives on PC through Steam on October 29, 2026. If you're deciding whether your group should pick it up, the practical questions are simple: does your machine meet the specs, how does the co-op actually work, and what does the Early Access label mean for you? The verified answers are all on the store page — and some details, like a confirmed price and Steam Deck status, are still open.

The game is developed and published by GamesMrkt, a small independent studio that says it has been building games since 2018. Fart Together is the studio's first Steam release.

## How the Co-op Works on PC

The core loop is coordination, not reflexes alone. Every player on the raft contributes force, direction, and timing, so the raft's movement is the sum of everyone's input. According to the developer's own description, the trick isn't farting harder — it's farting together at the right moment.

Steam lists the game with Online Co-Op as a feature, alongside Steam Cloud and Family Sharing. The store page tags it as a party game, multiplayer, physics, comedy, and team-based title, with a stylized, colorful, animal-themed presentation.

- **Players:** 2–4 in online co-op
- **Objective:** guide one shared raft through obstacle courses
- **Course hazards:** narrow passages, sharp turns, moving obstacles, and hazards that throw the raft off balance
- **Failure state:** one mistimed or misdirected input can send the whole raft off course

Because the raft responds to each player individually, the developer frames the game as one that "may test your friendships." That's marketing language, but it does describe the design: a single player reacting late can undo a clean run.

Worth noting: Steam's content section flags online interactivity, and the system requirements state that a stable broadband connection is required for online co-op. There is no verified information about local or split-screen play on the store page, so don't assume it.

## Early Access: What GamesMrkt Has Committed To

Fart Together launches directly into Early Access rather than as a finished 1.0 release. The developer's Steam FAQ lays out the plan in specific terms.

- **Duration:** approximately 2–3 months, with the caveat that the estimate may change based on testing and player feedback.
- **Planned additions:** more courses, obstacles, game modes, characters, and customization options, plus balance, performance, and polish work.
- **Pricing:** a lower price during Early Access, rising at full release, with Early Access owners receiving the full version at no extra cost.
- **Feedback channels:** Steam discussions, a Discord community, and player surveys.

A demo is also available to download from the store page, which is the lowest-risk way to check whether the raft controls click with your group before spending anything.

One thing to be clear about: at the time of writing, the store page does not display a confirmed price, and the genre field includes "Free To Play" alongside Early Access and other categories. Because the developer FAQ describes paid Early Access pricing, treat the cost as unconfirmed until the store page updates.
