## What Fart Together Actually Is

Fart Together is a physics-based obstacle course game from indie studio GamesMrkt, and it is scheduled to launch into Steam Early Access on October 29, 2026. Two to four players share one raft and steer it using fart power, coordinating direction, timing, and gas output to clear courses built around narrow passages, sharp turns, and moving hazards. The important limit for beginners: there is no solo campaign and no single-player mode described on the store page — this is online co-op only, so you need friends and a stable internet connection before you can learn anything.

The developer's own framing sets expectations nicely. As GamesMrkt puts it, the secret isn't farting harder — it's farting together at the right time. That single sentence is basically the whole beginner learning curve.

## Before Your First Run: Setup Checklist

Beginners lose more runs to setup problems than to bad steering, so handle this first.

- **Grab the free demo.** A playable demo is downloadable from the [official Fart Together Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5117550/Fart_Together/), which is the cheapest way to feel out the raft before committing.
- **Confirm your connection.** The store listing explicitly requires a stable broadband connection for online co-op.
- **Know your input options.** The game lists a Keyboard Only Option and support for Xbox and PlayStation controllers, so decide with your group who is using what before you start.
- **Check accessibility settings.** Listed features include camera comfort options, custom volume controls, and stereo sound — worth a look if motion-heavy physics games bother you.
- **Pick a language.** The interface is localized into English plus 16 more languages, including French, German, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, and Brazilian Portuguese.
- **Plan for cloud saves and sharing.** Steam Cloud and Family Sharing are both supported.

## How the Shared Raft Moves

Every player on the raft contributes force, and the raft responds to the direction and timing of each contribution. That means your input doesn't just move *you* — it moves everyone.

Three variables matter, according to the developer's description of the game:

1. **Direction** — which way the thrust pushes the raft.
2. **Timing** — when that push lands relative to everyone else's.
3. **Gas power** — how much force you're adding.

The physics are deliberately exaggerated rather than realistic. GamesMrkt openly jokes that the raft is only "sort of realistic," which is a useful beginner mindset: don't try to reason like a naval engineer. Instead, treat each course as a pattern to learn with your team.

## Beginner Tips That Match How the Game Works

These are grounded in what the store page describes about course design and co-op behavior.

- **Talk out loud, constantly.** The game is built around thinking together and acting at the right moment. Silence is the fastest way to drift off course.
- **Assign a caller.** Because one mistimed push can throw the whole raft, having one person say "now" or "hold" reduces chaos — especially in narrow passages.
- **Slow down before you speed up.** The developer notes that some sections require accelerating and others require easing off. Beginners tend to mash thrust and overshoot sharp turns.
- **Reset instead of arguing.** A run can be saved by a clean last-second maneuver, but a small mistake can also snowball. Agree in advance to restart quickly rather than debating blame mid-course.
- **Practice on the demo with the same group.** Timing chemistry is group-specific. Three friends who have run the demo together will outperform four strangers on launch day.
- **Expect moving obstacles to be the real test.** Hazards that throw the raft off balance punish late reactions more than wrong directions.

## Mistakes Nearly Every New Crew Makes

The store page is unusually candid about failure modes, which makes them easy to pre-empt.

- **One player reacting late.** A single delayed input can send the entire raft off course, and the group immediately starts questioning physics and each other.
- **Pushing at the wrong moment.** Raw power isn't the answer; synchronized power is.
- **Assuming someone else is steering.** In shared-control co-op, nobody is the driver unless your group decides that on purpose.
- **Blaming the physics.** The raft is intentionally unstable. Treat wobble as content, not a bug.

If your group includes someone with a questionable sense of direction or a habit of asking "What did I do?", the developer warns this game may drive you a little crazy. That's a feature, not a flaw — but plan your session length accordingly.

## What Early Access Means for Beginners

Fart Together is launching as an unfinished, evolving game, and that shapes how you should approach a beginner guide.

- GamesMrkt expects Early Access to last roughly **2–3 months**, while noting the estimate may shift with development progress and feedback.
- Planned full-version additions include more courses, obstacles, game modes, characters, and customization options, plus balance, performance, and polish work.
- Early Access owners are slated to receive the full release at no additional cost, and the developer plans to price Early Access lower than the finished game.
- Feedback is being collected through Steam discussions, a Discord community, and player surveys.

Practically, that means any course you master now could change. Learn the *habits* — communication, timing discipline, restrained thrust — rather than memorizing a specific obstacle layout.

## FAQ

**Is there a Fart Together beginner guide built into the game?**
Not according to the store listing. There is no tutorial mode described. Your best starting point is the free demo plus a group voice call, since the game's core skill is coordinated timing rather than memorizing inputs.

**Can I play Fart Together alone?**
No. The game is described as online co-op for 2–4 players steering a shared raft. There is no single-player mode mentioned, and a stable internet connection is required.

**Do I need a controller?**
Not necessarily. The accessibility features list a Keyboard Only Option, and Xbox and PlayStation controllers are also supported. Either input works as long as your group is consistent.

**How long will Fart Together stay in Early Access?**
GamesMrkt estimates about 2–3 months, with the caveat that the timeline may change based on testing and player feedback. More courses, modes, and characters are planned for the full version.

**Is Fart Together free?**
The store page lists the game under Early Access with a planned lower price during that period, and a free demo is available to download. Check the Steam listing for current pricing rather than relying on secondhand figures.

## The Bottom Line for New Crews

Fart Together rewards patience and communication far more than raw button-mashing. Download the demo, put your group in voice chat, pick one person to call the timing, and accept that your first several runs will end in a spectacular mess. That mess is the point — the developer built the game so you can laugh, shout, blame your friends, and eventually say "one more try."
