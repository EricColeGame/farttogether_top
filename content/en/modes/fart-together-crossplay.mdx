## The Short Answer on Fart Together Crossplay

If you're rounding up friends for launch day and some of them game on different hardware, the crossplay question comes up fast. As of October 29, 2026, **Fart Together crossplay is not confirmed anywhere in the game's official materials.** The [Fart Together page on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5117550/Fart_Together/) advertises online co-op for 2–4 players but never mentions cross-platform play, console versions, or cross-progression. Until GamesMrkt says otherwise, plan on everyone playing the PC version through Steam.

That's not a knock on the game. It's simply the honest state of the evidence, and knowing it before you buy saves you from a very awkward "wait, can you even join?" conversation on launch night.

### The Core Loop, Briefly

- Every player's input affects the whole raft, so one person farting at the wrong moment can wreck a run.
- Courses demand teamwork rather than raw speed — sometimes you accelerate, sometimes you brake.
- The developers lean into the chaos, warning that the game "may test your friendships."

## Which Platforms Can Play Fart Together?

Right now, the verified answer is Windows PC through Steam. The system requirements list Windows 10 64-bit at minimum and Windows 10/11 64-bit as recommended, with no macOS, Linux, or console builds mentioned.

A few details that get confused with platform support:

- **Controller support** — Xbox and PlayStation controllers are listed as supported input devices. That means you can plug in a PlayStation pad on PC; it does not mean PlayStation owners can join your raft.
- **Steam Deck** — compatibility is marked "Unknown," so handheld play is unverified rather than confirmed.
- **Demo** — a downloadable Fart Together demo exists on Steam, which is the safest way to check performance on your own machine before committing.

If a console version ever gets announced, crossplay becomes a fair question. Until then, there's nothing to connect.

## How to Play With Friends Without Crossplay

Since crossplay isn't on the table, the practical setup is straightforward: get everyone onto the same platform.

1. **Confirm everyone is on PC.** Each player needs Fart Together through Steam.
2. **Have friends wishlist the game** so they get notified the moment it unlocks.
3. **Test with the demo first** if you're unsure how a friend's PC will handle it.
4. **Use voice chat.** Coordination is the entire skill ceiling here, and calling out turns in real time beats typing.
5. **Check the connection.** The listing specifically calls out a stable broadband connection for online co-op, so wired or strong Wi-Fi is worth arranging before a session.

One caution: Steam Family Sharing is listed as a feature, but library sharing isn't the same thing as two people playing online co-op together at once. Don't build your group's plans around it.

## Could Crossplay Arrive During Early Access?

It's possible but not promised. GamesMrkt says Early Access should last roughly 2–3 months, and the studio's stated plans for the full version focus on **more courses, obstacles, game modes, characters, and customization options**, plus balance, performance, and polish work. The developers have said they'll gather feedback through Steam discussions, their Discord community, and player surveys, and that plans "may evolve." So if cross-platform play matters to you, the productive move is to ask for it in those channels rather than assume it's coming.

Worth noting: this is GamesMrkt's first Steam release, built in about two months of intense development. A small team shipping its debut in Early Access is likely to prioritize the content already on its roadmap before adding networking infrastructure across platforms.
