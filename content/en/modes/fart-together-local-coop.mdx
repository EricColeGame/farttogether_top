## Does Fart Together Have Local Co-op? The Short Answer

If you're hunting for **Fart Together local co-op** details, here's the honest answer: the game's official Steam listing does not mention local co-op, split-screen, or shared-screen play anywhere. What it does confirm is online co-op for 2–4 players, plus a note in the system requirements that a stable internet connection is required for online co-op.

That distinction matters before you buy. This is a teamwork game built entirely around players steering one shared raft, so the question of *how* you and your friends connect is basically the whole experience. As of the Early Access launch on October 29, 2026, the verified feature list points to online play only.

Fart Together is developed and published by GamesMrkt, a small independent studio that has been making games since 2018 and describes this as its first Steam release.

## How the Shared-Raft Co-op Loop Works

Understanding the core loop explains why connection method matters so much. Every player's input moves the same raft, so there is no solo lane and no private corner of the screen.

- **One raft, one direction.** A single player steering the wrong way can drag the whole team off course.
- **Timing over force.** The developers frame the skill ceiling as farting together at the right moment, not farting harder.
- **Course design punishes panic.** Narrow passages, sharp turns, moving obstacles, and hazards that knock the raft off balance all demand synchronized movement.
- **Mistakes are the comedy.** GamesMrkt openly markets the friendship-testing chaos: a clean maneuver can save a run, and one bad call can turn it into a disaster.

Because the raft reacts to each player's force, direction, and timing, voice communication becomes close to mandatory. That's an argument for online play with a headset — and, if you were hoping for couch play, it's the reason the missing local mode stings.

## If You Want Couch-Style Play, Here's Where Things Stand

There's no confirmed way to play Fart Together on a single screen with two controllers right now. A few realistic options:

1. **Play online with friends in the same room.** Each player joins from their own PC. It's not couch co-op, but it delivers the shouting-at-each-other experience the game is designed around.
2. **Check back after Early Access updates.** GamesMrkt has said the full version will add more courses, obstacles, game modes, characters, and customization options. Local play isn't on that list, so treat it as a possibility rather than a promise.
3. **Ask directly.** The developers say they collect feedback through Steam discussions, their Discord community, and player surveys. A request thread is the most direct route to finding out whether local co-op is even under consideration.

One more thing worth verifying yourself: Steam's Remote Play Together feature lets some multiplayer games be shared over the internet as a workaround for missing local modes. It is not mentioned on the current Fart Together listing, so don't count on it until the store page says otherwise.
