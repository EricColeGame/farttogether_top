## What Fart Together Online Multiplayer Actually Is

Fart Together's online multiplayer is not a side mode — it is the entire game. Two to four players climb onto a single shared raft and steer it through physics-based obstacle courses using fart power, which means nobody gets to drive alone. If you are searching for how the online co-op side of this game works before buying, the short answer is: you and up to three friends control one vessel together, coordinating direction, timing, and gas output, and the [official Fart Together Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5117550/Fart_Together/) lists Online Co-Op as a core feature. A stable internet connection is explicitly required for online co-op, per the game's own system requirements.

The game comes from GamesMrkt, a small independent studio that has been developing games since 2018. Fart Together is their first Steam release, built in roughly two months, and it is scheduled to enter Early Access on October 29, 2026.

## How Co-Op Works: Steering One Raft as a Team

The loop is simple to describe and much harder to execute. Every player's input pushes the shared raft, and the raft reacts to the force, direction, and timing of each individual. That single design decision turns a goofy premise into a genuine teamwork problem.

Courses are built to punish drift and reward synchronization:

- **Narrow passages** that require the whole team to hold a straight line
- **Sharp turns** that need a coordinated burst rather than four conflicting ones
- **Moving obstacles** that force the group to react on the same beat
- **Hazards that throw the raft off balance**, turning a clean run into a scramble

The developers put the design philosophy bluntly: the secret is not farting harder, it is farting together at the right time. Sometimes the correct call is to speed up. Sometimes it is to slow down. Sometimes it is for all four players to act on the exact same frame.

### Why Timing Beats Raw Power

Because the raft is shared, an uncoordinated big push is worse than a small coordinated one. One player going the wrong way, reacting late, or firing at the wrong moment can send the entire raft off course — and the store page cheerfully warns that this may test your friendships. Treat the raft as a single body with four independent opinions, and the physics start making sense.

### Communication Is a Mechanic, Not a Nicety

There is no listed ping system, emote wheel, or in-game callout feature on the store page, so voice chat or a separate party call is the practical way to line up maneuvers. Before your first course, agree on simple verbal cues for "hold straight," "hard left," and "everyone now." Ambiguity is the real final boss.

## Tips for a Smoother Online Multiplayer Run

These are practical suggestions rather than documented game features, but they follow directly from how the shared-raft physics are described:

1. **Do a warm-up course first.** Learn how much force one player's input actually generates before you attempt precision sections as a group.
2. **Assign a caller.** One person verbally counts the group into simultaneous maneuvers. It cuts down on overlapping, conflicting inputs.
3. **Slow before you squeeze.** Narrow passages punish momentum, so bleed speed early instead of correcting mid-gap.
4. **Review the wipe, not the blame.** After a failed run, name the specific mistimed input. "We turned too early" is actionable; "you messed up" is not.
5. **Keep sessions short.** Coordination-heavy party games get sloppy fast, and this one is explicitly designed to frustrate.

## FAQ

**Is Fart Together online multiplayer or local co-op?**
The Steam listing specifically features Online Co-Op, and the system requirements call out a stable internet connection for online co-op. Local or split-screen play is not mentioned on the store page, so treat couch co-op as unconfirmed until the developers say otherwise.

**How many players can join a Fart Together online multiplayer session?**
Two to four players. Everyone shares one raft, so a two-player run is more forgiving on coordination while a four-player run multiplies both the chaos and the force applied to the raft.

**Do I need a strong PC to play online co-op?**
No. The minimum spec is an Intel Core i5-8400 or AMD Ryzen 3 1200 with 8 GB of RAM and a GTX 1050 Ti or RX 560, plus 4 GB of storage. The bigger factor is connection stability, which the developers list as a requirement for online play.

**Does Fart Together support cross-play between platforms?**
Cross-play is not mentioned anywhere on the store page. The game is listed for Windows PC via Steam, and Steam Deck status is marked Unknown, so plan on a PC-to-PC group for now.

**Is it worth buying during Early Access?**
If you want the current courses at the planned lower Early Access price and are comfortable with an unfinished game that will change, yes — Early Access buyers get the full release at no extra cost. If you would rather play a polished version, the developers themselves suggest waiting.
