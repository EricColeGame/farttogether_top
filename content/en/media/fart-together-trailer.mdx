## What the Fart Together Trailer Shows

The trailer is a gameplay trailer, not a cinematic teaser, and it lines up with the pitch on the store page: a physics-based co-op obstacle course where **2–4 players** steer one shared raft using fart power.

Based on the developer's own description of the game, the footage corresponds to these confirmed mechanics:

- **Shared control.** Every player influences the same raft, so direction, timing, and "gas power" have to be coordinated.
- **Timing over force.** The developers state the secret is not farting harder, but farting together at the right moment.
- **Course hazards.** Narrow passages, sharp turns, moving obstacles, and hazards that knock the raft off balance.
- **Speed changes.** Some sections call for speeding up, others for slowing down, and some require the whole team to move at once.
- **Hippo characters.** The studio mentions testing "hippo farts" during development, which matches the animal-themed, colorful, stylized tags on the store page.

The page also stresses that a single player going the wrong way or reacting late can send the whole raft off course — the trailer's job is to sell that chaos.
