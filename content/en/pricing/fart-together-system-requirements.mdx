## Fart Together System Requirements: The Short Answer

Fart Together is a lightweight physics party game, so the bar to run it is low. According to the [official Fart Together Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5117550/Fart_Together/), you need a 64-bit Windows 10 machine, 8 GB of RAM, a GTX 1050 Ti or RX 560-class GPU, DirectX 11, 4 GB of storage, and a broadband connection for online co-op. The recommended tier bumps you to 16 GB of RAM and a GTX 1660 or RX 590.

Why it matters: the game is built around 2–4 players steering one shared raft together, so a stuttering PC or a shaky connection doesn't just hurt you — it wrecks the run for everyone on board. Here's the full breakdown, plus what the Early Access pricing plan means for your wallet.

## Fart Together System Requirements at a Glance

Both tiers require a 64-bit processor and operating system, DirectX version 11, a DirectX-compatible sound card, and 4 GB of available space.

| Component | Minimum | Recommended |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OS | Windows 10 64-bit | Windows 10/11 64-bit |
| Processor | Intel Core i5-8400 or AMD Ryzen 3 1200 | Intel Core i5-10400 or AMD Ryzen 5 3600 |
| Memory | 8 GB RAM | 16 GB RAM |
| Graphics | NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti or AMD Radeon RX 560 | NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 or AMD Radeon RX 590 |
| DirectX | Version 11 | Version 11 |
| Network | Broadband Internet connection | Broadband Internet connection |
| Storage | 4 GB available space | 4 GB available space |
| Sound Card | DirectX compatible | DirectX compatible |

The developer's own note on both tiers is blunt: a stable internet connection is required for online co-op. There is no listed offline or local-play spec, so plan around a live connection.

## What the Minimum Specs Actually Mean for You

The minimum tier is genuinely modest — an i5-8400 or Ryzen 3 1200 paired with a GTX 1050 Ti or RX 560 is older entry-level hardware. If you bought a budget gaming PC or laptop in the last several years, you very likely clear this bar.

A few practical checks before you buy:

- **Confirm 64-bit Windows.** Windows 10 64-bit is the floor; 32-bit installs are not supported at all.
- **Check your free drive space.** 4 GB is small, but leave headroom for save data, shader caches, and patches during Early Access.
- **Verify your GPU, not just the name.** Laptop versions of these cards can perform below their desktop counterparts, so compare against the desktop models listed.
- **Update DirectX and your drivers.** DirectX 11 is required, and a current GPU driver avoids the most common launch-day headaches.

## What the Recommended Specs Buy You

The recommended tier roughly doubles the memory to 16 GB and moves to an i5-10400 or Ryzen 5 3600 with a GTX 1660 or RX 590. Nothing here is demanding by modern standards, which fits the game's stylized, colorful 3D presentation.

Since Fart Together is a physics-driven co-op game where the raft reacts to force, direction, and timing from each player, a smoother frame rate makes split-second coordination easier to read. That's the practical argument for the recommended tier rather than the minimum — not raw visual fidelity.

## Internet, Steam Features, and Steam Deck Status

Because the game is online co-op for 2–4 players, your connection matters as much as your GPU. The store listing flags broadband internet as required for both tiers, and the page carries an "online interactivity" content notice.

Other verified store details worth knowing:

- **Features:** Online Co-Op, Steam Cloud, and Family Sharing are all listed.
- **Steam Deck:** Compatibility is listed as **Unknown** — Valve has not rated it as Verified or Playable, so don't assume handheld support on day one.
- **Languages:** Interface support for English plus 16 more, including French, German, Spanish (Spain), Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Polish, Russian, Turkish, Arabic, Dutch, Vietnamese, and Persian. Only interface translation is listed — no full audio or subtitle columns are marked.

## Price, Early Access, and What You're Actually Paying For

Fart Together is scheduled to launch into Early Access on October 29, 2026, developed and published by GamesMrkt. On pricing, the developer's Steam FAQ is specific about the plan but not about a number: they intend to charge **less during Early Access** and raise the price at full release, with Early Access owners receiving the full version at no extra cost.

A couple of honest caveats:

- **No confirmed dollar amount.** The store page does not display a firm price, and its genre tags include a "Free To Play" label that sits awkwardly next to the paid Early Access FAQ. Treat any specific figure you see elsewhere as unconfirmed until Steam lists it.
- **A demo exists.** A "Download Fart Together Demo" option is on the page, which is the cheapest possible way to test whether your rig handles the raft physics before spending anything.
- **Early Access is expected to last roughly 2–3 months**, per the developer, though they note that estimate can shift with testing and feedback.

If your PC clears the minimum specs, the demo plus the lower Early Access price is the low-risk path. If you're near the bottom of the minimum tier, wait for performance reports from other players before committing.

## FAQ

**Can I run Fart Together system requirements on a laptop?**
Possibly, if the laptop has a dedicated GPU at or above the GTX 1050 Ti / RX 560 level, 8 GB of RAM, 64-bit Windows 10, and 4 GB of free space. Integrated graphics aren't listed as supported, and laptop GPUs can trail their desktop equivalents.

**Does Fart Together need an internet connection to play?**
Yes for online co-op — the store page lists broadband internet as required at both the minimum and recommended tiers, with a note that a stable connection is needed for online play.

**How much storage does Fart Together take?**
4 GB of available space at both the minimum and recommended tiers, which is one of the smallest footprints you'll see for a 3D multiplayer game.

**Is Fart Together coming to Steam Deck?**
Steam Deck compatibility is currently marked Unknown on the store page, so Valve hasn't confirmed it as Verified or Playable. Check the listing again closer to launch.
