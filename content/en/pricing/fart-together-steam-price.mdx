## How GamesMrkt Says Early Access Pricing Will Work

The developer's Early Access FAQ answers the pricing question in plain language. According to GamesMrkt, the plan is to **charge less during Early Access and raise the price at full release** as more content and features arrive. The studio also commits that anyone who buys in during Early Access receives the full version at no extra cost.

That's a meaningful detail for anyone deciding whether to wait. Buying early is framed as the cheaper entry point, not a discount that expires — the content grows, but your purchase doesn't.

GamesMrkt estimates Early Access will run roughly **two to three months**, though the studio notes that timeline could shift based on testing and player feedback. If the price rises at 1.0, that window is your window.

### What Ships in the Early Access Build

The current version is a physics-based co-op obstacle course for **two to four players** steering a single shared raft. Each player's fart applies force in a direction, and the raft responds to the combined timing and power of the group. Courses include narrow passages, sharp turns, and moving hazards designed to break your coordination.

The full release is planned to add more courses, obstacles, game modes, characters, and customization options, along with balance and performance work. None of those additions carry an extra charge for Early Access owners.
