## Why the Free Demo Is Worth Your Time (and Your Friendships)

GamesMrkt is upfront that the game is designed to cause arguments. The store copy warns that one player going the wrong way, reacting late, or firing at the wrong moment can send the whole raft off course — and that coordination is "a requirement," not a suggestion.

The demo is where you find out which of your friends that describes.

Mechanically, the raft responds to the force, direction, and timing of each player's input. Courses throw narrow passages, sharp turns, moving obstacles, and hazards that knock the raft off balance. The studio's own framing of the skill curve is worth repeating: the secret isn't farting harder, it's farting together at the right time.

Reviews back up the pitch. Of 757 user reviews at the time of writing, 89% are positive — a strong signal for a two-month prototype from a small studio.
