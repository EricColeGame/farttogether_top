## What You Get for the Money During Early Access

Paying during Early Access means paying for an unfinished game, and Steam says so plainly. The page carries the standard warning that Early Access titles are incomplete and may change. Here is what the current build is documented to include:

- Physics-based obstacle courses for 2–4 players steering one shared raft
- Online co-op with Steam Cloud support and Family Sharing enabled
- Narrow passages, sharp turns, moving hazards, and timing-based maneuvers
- Interface support for English plus 16 additional languages
- Controller support for Xbox and PlayStation pads, plus a keyboard-only option

The developer estimates the Early Access period will run approximately 2–3 months, though that estimate may shift with testing and player feedback. Family Sharing is worth flagging: if a household member already owns the game, Steam's sharing feature can stretch the value of a single purchase.
